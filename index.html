Online Attendenc management System

Online Attendance Management System is a simple and secure web-based platform designed to manage student attendance digitally. Teachers can record attendance easily, while students can view their attendance anytime.

About

Our Online Attendance Management System helps educational institutions manage student attendance in a simple, organized, and efficient way. It reduces manual paperwork and makes attendance records easier to maintain, access, and monitor.

Featue

1. Secure student and teacher login

2. Easy manual attendance marking

3. Student-wise attendance records

4. Subject-wise attendance

5. Overall attendance percentage

6. Attendance reports

7. Easy-to-use dashboard

8. Secure and organized data management

How It Work

1. Teacher Login Teacher logs into the system using their account.

2. Select Class & Subject Teacher selects the required class, section, and subject.

3. Mark Attendance Teacher marks each student as Present or Absent.

4. Save Attendance The attendance record is securely saved in the system.

5. Student Views Attendance Students can log in and check their subject-wise and overall attendance.
